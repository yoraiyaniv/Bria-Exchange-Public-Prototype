Claim verdicts
Every claim checked, individually

Bria Exchange decomposes any text into atomic claims and verifies each one against domain-appropriate licensed sources - whether you wrote it, your AI generated it, or you received it from someone else. Every claim comes back corroborated, contradicted, or unsupported - with the citation that explains why.

Correction guidance
Not just what's wrong - what's right

For every contradicted or unsupported claim, Bria Exchange returns a suggested correction grounded in the source evidence. Useful whether you're fixing an AI draft before publishing or deciding how much weight to give a report you just received.

Pass · Review · Block
Efficient human review, at scale

Bria Exchange applies a configurable policy to route each response - passing high-confidence outputs automatically, flagging borderline cases to a human review queue, and blocking outputs that fall below your thresholds. Your team reviews only what actually needs them.

Audit log
Defensibility built in from the start

Every verification event is logged with the full claim-level trace - verdict, source, citation, policy decision, and any human review outcome. Filterable, exportable as CSV, and built for regulated environments where you need to show your work.
